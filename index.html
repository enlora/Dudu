A Note Just for You

Dayan

I’m glad you’re still in my life. Things shifted between us, and that’s okay. What stayed is simple: I care about you, I root for you, and I’m here if you ever need someone who will listen.

So on this birthday, I just want you to know this, quietly and without needing anything back:

You will always have someone in your corner.

Someone who wants good things for you. Someone who notices. Someone who cares. That doesn’t change with the year.

Happy birthday, Dudu. May this year be gentle with you. May it bring you rest, good people, and days that feel light.

— Naomi
